# Approved Project Decisions

## D001 — Native Android
**Approved.** V1 is native Android.

## D002 — Authentication
**Approved.** Mobile + OTP. No guest mode. Name/language are profile data.

## D003 — Voice
**Approved.** Voice is mandatory in V1. Turn-based only.

## D004 — Incident Vault
**Approved.** User chooses Save or Not Now. Conversation and incident are separate.

## D005 — Local Evidence
**Approved.** V1 does not require cloud evidence storage.

## D006 — OCR
**Approved.** OCR/document intelligence are V2.

## D007 — Legal Domains
**Approved.** Police, Traffic, Railway, Government/RTS, Cyber.

## D008 — RAG
**Approved.** PostgreSQL + pgvector through LegalKnowledgeService.

## D009 — AI Architecture
**Approved.** Modular AI orchestrator; no complex autonomous multi-agent V1.

## D010 — AI Provider
**Approved.** Provider abstraction is mandatory.

## D011 — Cloud Documents
**Deferred.** Optional secure cloud backup is V2.

## D012 — Scalability
**Approved.** Modular boundaries now; microservice extraction only when justified.

## D013 — Positioning
**Approved.** Legal First-Aid for Everyday Situations.

## D014 — Quality
**Approved.** At least 100+ legal scenario evaluations before public launch.

## Change policy
A decision change must state:
1. old decision
2. new decision
3. reason
4. impact
5. affected files

Then update this ledger.
