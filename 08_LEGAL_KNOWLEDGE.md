# Legal Knowledge Base Specification

## V1 domains
### Police
Stops, station requests, notices, questioning, arrest, search, seizure, complaint/FIR refusal, misconduct, cash demands, assault, witness interactions.

### Traffic
Helmet, seatbelt, signals, speeding, phone use, challans, towing, seizure, licence, insurance, PUC, RC, parking, accident, cash demand.

### Railway
Ticketless travel, wrong ticket/class, UTS, season pass, penalties, excess fare, TC interaction, RPF/GRP, theft, harassment, assault.

### Government/RTS
Application delay/rejection, refusal to accept, additional documents, unofficial payment, certificates, public-service grievances, RTS, RTI, appeals.

### Cyber
UPI/bank/card fraud, OTP scams, fake support, investment/loan/job/shopping scams, phishing, compromised accounts, SIM fraud, impersonation, blackmail, sextortion, threats, image misuse, data leaks.

## Preferred authoritative source hierarchy
1. India Code

4. Official traffic systems
5. Indian Railways

7. Western Railway

9. RPF

14. Bombay High Court
15. Legal Services Authorities
16. other official departments/regulators

## Source record
Record title, official URL, issuer, jurisdiction, effective date, verification status, last verification, reviewer and version/status.

## Current law
The corpus must account for:
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023
- Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023
- Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023
and applicable rules, notifications and transitional provisions.

Do not assume older criminal-law references remain operative without checking applicability.

## Important
This file defines the knowledge model and quality rules; it is not itself a substitute for current legal verification.
